Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information across our document and image conversion platform. We operate on a Privacy-by-Design principle, ensuring that data minimization is at the core of our technical architecture.

I. Data Processing & The "Zero-Retention" Protocol

  • User Content: We process uploaded documents (e.g., PDF, DOCX) and images (e.g., PNG, JPEG) solely to perform the requested conversion or compression.
  • Temporary Storage: Files are stored in a volatile, encrypted temporary directory during processing.
  • Automated Deletion: All uploaded and output files are automatically and permanently purged from our servers within one (1) hour.
  • No Access: Our staff and third-party automated systems do not "read," "mine," or "train AI" on your uploaded content.

II. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is required to fulfill the service you have initiated.
  • Legitimate Interest: We collect non-identifiable technical data (IP address, browser type) for cybersecurity and server load balancing.

III. Data Security & International Transfers

  • Encryption: All data in transit is protected by encryption.
  • Global Transfers: Your data may be processed on servers located outside your home country; however, we ensure data protection via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

IV. Your Rights

Under global regulations, you have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of any personal data we hold. Given our one-hour deletion policy, we typically do not hold any personal data after your session ends.
